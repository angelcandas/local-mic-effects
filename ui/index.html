Split into stems

Separates the song into vocals, drums, bass and everything else, each on its own track under the song. It runs on your computer and takes a few minutes for a 4-minute song. The first time, it downloads the separation model (about 84 MB).

Auto Mix

Listens to your voice, the backing and any instruments, then adds the effects each track needs (EQ, compressor, de-esser, reverb) and sets the levels. One undo takes it all back.
